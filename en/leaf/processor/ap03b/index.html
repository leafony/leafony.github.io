Features

This MCU (microcontroller) leaf runs programs to read sensors and control communication leaves and other modules. It is a redesigned version of AP03 STM32 MCU, using the STMicroelectronics STM32L452REI6.

It connects to other leaves through the Leafony Bus and operates from 3.3V. The board includes a PG switch for BOOT0, a reset switch, and an LED indicating the BOOT0 state. It is the MCU leaf in the Cellular LPWA Cat-M1 Kit.

Power and external connections

Supply the Leafony Bus 3.3V terminal from a power leaf or other suitable source. The VBUS terminal is not connected to the AP03B circuitry. There is no USB connector on the board; provide a separate interface for a computer or debugger.

SDA and SCL each have a 10 kΩ pull-up resistor to 3.3V. Before connecting external devices, check the Leafony Bus specification and the other device’s power and signal voltages.

AREF connection resistor R5 is DNP (not populated), so the bus AREF terminal is not connected to the MCU’s analog supply/reference circuit in the standard configuration.

Switches and LED

ComponentBoard markingFunction
SW1PGSelects BOOT0 high / low.
SW2RSTConnects RESET to GND when pressed.
D1D1Lights when BOOT0 is high.

The LED indicates the BOOT0 state, not program execution or a successful upload. For boot-mode details, check the MCU configuration and the reset / program selection circuit.

D9 switching by BOOT0

Analog switch U2 is between D9 and PA10. They connect when BOOT0 is low and disconnect when it is high. Check the PG switch when combining the board with a communication leaf that uses D9. D0, D1, D6 and D7 connect directly to the MCU ports listed above.
